Project Charter

This will be the project of university based pandemic preparedness research lab in the University of the Fraser Valley. Its name will be “Fraser Valley Preparedness Research Lab”. Its first purpose will be whether the clinic manager can make a better decision to increase or decrease the staff shifts based on the need. This lab will collect the data and then make patterns and do calculations to figure it out, how much staff we will need from initial phase of the pandemic. (BC Centre for Disease Control, 2026b)

In addition to this, lab can be able to calculate that how early to open extra capacity, which sites can share supplies, and whether the response preserves access in smaller communities. (BC Centre for Disease Control, 2026b)

Second purpose of this lab will be to do research to find better solutions for the next pandemic if happen. For example, doing research with the use of modern technologies like Artificial Intelligence and integration of it in finding the protein structures and finding the weak points of the viruses as soon as possible, so that scientists can find the solution to attack that weak point and then kill the virus at mass scale.

The invitation to the Garry Fehr can be considered in this strategic planning materials because he has vast experience in the managing the projects and succession of the project will not be dependent on one person. (University of the Fraser Valley, n.d.-b)

Dr. Garry Fehr (University of the Fraser Valley, n.d.-b)

This project would close on December 31, 2029, with an accepted operating service. Ultimately, this lab's contribution is earlier detection, testing protective measures, fewer interruptions in the service of the clinics when the threat of the pandemic will reach the Fraser region. (BC Centre for Disease Control, 2026b)

Scope

It will start with two willing clinical service sites for discovery and shadow testing, and both sites will be selected for different operations. Then we will ask each site to name a decision, its deadline, the person able to act and the evidence currently available that will support their decision.

The research software that will be part of this project, it will be used to store the data of the decisions taken, evidence behind those decisions, and regional support service actions taken.

Why The Fraser Valley Needs Its Own Preparedness Research

During an outbreak, a manager in Hope must decide whether the enough people can reach the clinic and whether the next shift can handle the number of patients that are expected to arrive, which will be predicted by our research software.

The 2021 census counted 324,005 people in the Fraser Valley and it is up 9.5% from 295,934 in 2016. This increasing population in the Fraser Valley reason tells that there is need of high investment to predict the future patient counts visiting the clinics so that protective measures can be taken. For example, supplying the medicines, equipment at right time, right place and in right amount. These protective measures can save many lives. (Statistics Canada, 2022a)

It includes what the project should deliver

What Will Be the Purpose of The Local Alert

British Columbia is already publishing respiratory surveillance dashboards and weekly summaries. Recent summary illustrates the reason that why the lab should preserve several indicators. For example, during COVID-19 pandemic, COVID-19 test positives had been increasing while the proportions of emergency and primary care visits for respiratory illness remained low. These are the things that needs to be observed as soon as possible, and correct data should be collected and presented. (BC Centre for Disease Control, 2026b)

Meanwhile, wastewater can provide additional information about the community. For example, wastewater can be tested at regular intervals so that UFV lab will get the data about the viruses and bacteria, which bacteria and viruses are growing, and which viruses are new to humans, can be detected early. (BC Centre for Disease Control, 2026b)

Research Software

How the lab will recognize an unusual rise in illness

A Fraser Valley clinic manager needs to know whether a busy waiting room reflects an emerging regional problem, a nearby clinic closure or the usual winter pattern.

This lab will use different types of data to feed in the research software; it will compare respiratory visits with the total number of visits. It will also include the data like the holidays, days of the week, season, and other events throughout the year, so that number of patients visiting can be compared with these events. (BC Centre for Disease Control, 2026a)

 

Lab will use AI also to detect the unusual patterns and other instant spikes in the visits. With the integration of the AI, data analyzation will be faster, and decisions can be taken quicker by the clinic's manager.

The second part of the software would predict the number of visits in clinic could expected during the next week. This will also include the number of supplies that will be needed in that clinic for the next week, and it will also take the data from the schedule supply ordering of that clinic and then this software can predict the amount of the supplies needed for the next week. (Fox et al., 2024)

Biological Border

A biological border is needed to build where residents already share indoor air. This can be done by monitoring the air regularly with the use of sensors and it will detect the viruses and meanwhile new viruses that are not known to humans. (World Health Organization, 2021)

To make this possible, there is need to install sensors in every residential building and workplace and that sensors will send data via WIFI to the lab and it will be monitored and continuously analyzed with the AI and Research software, when it will be detected a foreign virus then an alert like alarm will be sounded to alert the people in that building. In addition to this, there will proposed regulations to increase the ventilation so that effect of the viruses will be decreased. (World Health Organization, 2021)

Pharmaceutical Research Partnerships

Partnership with Eli Lilly will focus on the methods improve the data of the research software so that model predictions accuracy can be increased. Other partnerships with Pfizer and AstraZeneca regarding the respiratory care. With these companies the development of the vaccines can be accelerated by sharing the real time data with them and they can do their research and then vaccine for the new virus can be invented as soon as possible. (Pfizer, n.d.)

Management

Three Spheres Model for Systems Management

  1. Business sphere include for example, by adding the extra shift of a nurse at the clinic will cover the appointments or not. (Renso, 2026b)
  2. Organizational sphere include that who have the authority to approve the overtime and organize the team in the need. (Renso, 2026b)
  3. Technology sphere include that if the research software will deliver the predicted data on time, with accuracy, and with clarity to the clinics. (Renso, 2026b)

Four Organizational Frames

  1. The structural frame shows that who will make which decision, like UFV will do the research, while the clinics will send the data of their patients visits.
  2. The human resource frame will include the training and workshops done by the trainers to train the nurses at the clinics for sending the data to UFV lab. Then ask trainees that whether they could use that time. (Renso, 2026b).
  3. The political frame will include, when the two clinics will need the same mobile healthcare team at the same time. This will reveal the political side of this project. So, there is need to set the clear rules to avoid these types of conflicts.
  4. The symbolic frame shows the real actions, like listening to remote staff and addressing their real issues and solving those. So, this will build the trust. Pasting the logo on the website will not affect the relationship strengths with those remote clinics so, active listening will be needed. (Renso, 2026b)

The Champion, Pre-Initiation and Authorization

The primary champion are the University of the Fraser Valley executives and Dr. Garry Fehr so that the cross-department decisions can be secured effectively.

Pre-Initiative include the key baselines like regional problems, deciding the spending ceiling, and the sponsors. Meanwhile, purchasing officer will uses the approved charter and then prepare the procurement packages within the budget.

Project Life Cycle Model

For the development of the research software, spiral model will be used. Each cycle will initiate with the decision, and one uncertainty will be decided. Cycles will evaluate weekly count delivery, forecast complexity, and tool performance during simulated shortages.

Five Project Management Process Groups

  1. Initiating: this phase will confirm that lab has the necessary permissions to start developing the software and start other parts of the project.
  2. Planning: this phase will include the details of the work that needs to be done, how much it will cost and who will be responsible for it.
  3. Executing: in the phase, teams will be built and resources will be collected. Then development of the research software and installation of the sensors will be started.
  4. Monitoring and Controlling: this phase checks the progress of the project and prepare the status reports and progress reports. Then quality and other problems will be fixed and documented.
  5. Closing: in the phase the development of the project will be finished, and then final cost will be calculated, lessons learned from the mistakes will be recorded, the work will be transferred to the people who will finalize the project and its reports. (Renso, 2026b, Class 2 lecture slides)

Progress Reports and Status Reports

Each team leader will submit the weekly progress report, and it will include the reporting date, completed milestones, next decision that will be taken and by who, and the deadline for that decision. (Renso, 2026)

Meanwhile, the Finance department lead will send the spending report and comparison with the approved budget.

Along with this, a status reports will also be sent by all leads to get the status of the project at a specific time.

This process includes recording the request of the stakeholder and reason behind that request. Then effects of that request will be reviewed on the cost, privacy, and the existing project work. After this, the change authority will approve the request and then update the scope, schedule, budget, testing and requirements and everyone will be informed.

SWOT Analysis

Strengths

The lab has a clear focus in solving the practical and real-life problems that are associated with the pandemic. Meanwhile, researchers and clinic managers take the better decisions by using that information about the staff schedules, supplies, and emergency planning. (Renso, 2026)

Weaknesses

First one is that the lab is depends on the information that will be provided by other organizations. If many organizations will provide the data, then different reporting methods can create the inconsistent results, which will lead to errors. (Renso, 2026)

Opportunities

Lab can use the same data and the research software access to other clinics in other regions and then each regions' clinic can make their own decisions based on the predictions by this software. (Renso, 2026)

Threats

A participating clinic can leave the project and stop sending the data to UFV lab in between and that can slow down the progress. So, this project should prepare first the smaller version that will only collect the data from one clinic. (Renso, 2026)

Return on investment (ROI) / payback analysis

Return on investment and chances of the better investments can be done by first reviewing the data and the reporting data methods that are being used in the research software.

Secondly, by delaying the expensive equipment purchases and can only be bought when the reliable information about that equipment will be available. Third, by testing the situation that if one clinic will not send the data or stop sending in between then what will be the effect of it on the research software development. Then board members can use the SWOT analysing to enhance the investment decisions.

By considering all these things, payback analysis can be done, and better decisions can be taken.

Project Quality Management

Quality Planning

It can be done by deciding what the report will contain and when it is needed, then setting the clear requirements, then assigning to someone who is responsible to check that whether these requirements are met or not.

Quality Assurance

This is done by checking whether the team are following the correct procedures, then review the document and changes in it. 

Quality Control

By testing each software release, identify and record defects, and make sure the final product works correctly.

Testing

This is done by checking the results of the calculations that it should produce the correct results, no duplicates of the records, no missing information, and then asking the clinic managers that the result reports are easy to process and read.

Cost Of Quality

Cost can be decreased by training the staff to enter the information correctly, and review the quality of the information, fixing the errors before the report release and after the report release to improve further.

Quality Incident Management

These quality related incidents will be recorded for future lessons, problems will be written in it and the cause of those problems, and then quality checklists will be made to set the future practices.
